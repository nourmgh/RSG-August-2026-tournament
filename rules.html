Tournament rules

TeamsBracketRules

1. Tournament Format

  • 9 teams participate.
  • 5-team League 1 and 4-team League 2.
  • Each team plays 4 League Stage games.
  • Each team plays 2 Crossover Stage games, for 6 games total.
  • All nine teams are combined into one overall standings table.
  • Top four advance to semifinals: #1 vs #4 and #2 vs #3.
  • Semifinal winners advance to the final.

2. Game Format

  • League and crossover games: 20 minutes.
  • Semifinals: 20 minutes.
  • Final: 30 minutes.
  • Teams should be ready at their assigned field before kickoff.

3. Standings

  • Win = 3 points
  • Draw = 1 point
  • Loss = 0 points

Tiebreakers:

  1. Points
  2. Goal differential
  3. Goals scored
  4. Head-to-head, if applicable
  5. Additional organizer tiebreaker if needed

4. Knockout Stage

  • Top 4 qualify.
  • Semifinals: #1 vs #4 and #2 vs #3.
  • Tied semifinals go directly to penalty kicks.
  • Tied final goes directly to penalty kicks.
